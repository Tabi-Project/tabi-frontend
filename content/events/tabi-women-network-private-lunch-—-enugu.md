  Tabi Women Network is bringing together a carefully curated circle of women executives, founders, senior leaders and changemakers across tech, business and governance for an intimate private lunch in Enugu. This is not a conference, a panel, or a networking event in the traditional sense. It is a table — warm, intentional, and built for real conversation.

  The goal is simple: to create a space where women who are building, leading and shaping industries across Africa can meet, exchange ideas, identify opportunities to collaborate and build relationships that go beyond the afternoon.

  This inaugural Enugu gathering is the beginning of something much larger. Tabi Women Network holds these intimate lunches every month across cities and countries across Africa. Every woman who joins becomes part of a growing pan-African directory of women making impact — an accessible, living network where members can find the right people, spot the right opportunities and build real partnerships across borders.

  By invitation only. Limited to 15 women.
