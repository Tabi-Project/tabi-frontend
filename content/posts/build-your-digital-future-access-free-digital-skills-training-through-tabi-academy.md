\
**The future of work is digital, and access to the right skills can make all the difference.**
At **Tabi Academy**, we believe that access to education should open doors to opportunities, regardless of gender or background. Through free technology education and initiatives that create pathways for people to participate in the digital economy, we continue to champion a future where more people can learn, grow, and thrive.
\
That is why we are excited to share an opportunity made possible through the **Gateway × CcHUB × Mastercard Foundation** initiative, designed to help more people access digital skills training.

Whether you are a student, a recent graduate, an aspiring creative, an entrepreneur, or someone looking to improve your career prospects, this opportunity could be your next step towards a more digitally empowered future.

**What Can You Learn?**

The program offers learners the opportunity to develop skills in areas that support creativity, business growth, and digital career development.

Available learning areas include:

* **Digital Marketing:**

   Learn how digital platforms can help businesses reach audiences, build brands, and achieve marketing objectives.
* **UI/UX Design:**

   Explore the principles of creating intuitive, accessible, and user-friendly digital experiences.
* **Graphics Design:**

   Develop creative skills for communicating ideas visually across digital and other media.
* **Video Production and Editing:**

   Learn how to create and edit engaging video content for different audiences and platforms.       

  **More Than Free Training: Support for Your Learning Journey**
* One of the highlights of this opportunity is the provision of a 

  **₦1,500 daily stipend for eligible learners**

   to support learning-related expenses, including data costs.
* This support can help reduce some of the financial barriers that prevent people from participating in digital education.
* Combined with access to free training, the stipend makes this an opportunity worth exploring if you are ready to invest your time and effort in developing valuable skills.

However, remember that learning is an active process. The greatest benefit comes from showing up consistently, practising what you learn, and applying your knowledge to real projects.
