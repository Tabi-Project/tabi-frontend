bio: >-
  Sophia is a passionate advocate for women's empowerment and the Director at
  Tabi (TEE Foundation), where she leads and manages programs, designs and
  implements transformative initiatives, and champions products and solutions
  built to uplift and reshape the lives of women. With a sharp eye for impact
  and a heart for purpose-driven work, she brings both strategic vision and
  hands-on leadership to everything she builds at Tabi — from growing a
  dedicated team to creating initiatives that open real doors for women in
  meaningful ways.


  Her work is rooted in the belief that when women are empowered, entire communities are transformed.



  When she's not changing lives through her work, Sophia is out living fully. An actress, visual artist, and photographer, she tells stories in every medium available to her. She feeds her curiosity by travelling the world, immersing herself in new cultures, and picking up languages along the way — she speaks French and Spanish, and is probably already eyeing the next one. You'll also find her buried in a great book or deep into a video game, equally happy losing herself in a rich narrative whether it's on a page or a screen.
